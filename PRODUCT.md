# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People with a working printer and a missing or empty black cartridge: home offices, students and small offices in a hurry. They usually have one colored cartridge left, a document due now, and no appetite for installing software or uploading sensitive pages to a random website.

## Product Purpose

A local-first PDF and image tool that turns black ink into the color that is actually available. It also removes passwords from PDFs. Success is a person dropping in a file, seeing the preview change color, downloading it and printing without ever wondering where the file went.

## Brand Personality

Precise, quiet, technical. It should feel like a well-made developer tool that happens to print documents, not a cheerful office utility. Confidence comes from exact type, hairline structure, real previews and honest copy.

## Anti-references

Template SaaS gradients, gradient headlines, glassmorphism, neon cyberpunk, floating blobs, oversized marketing heroes, identical icon-card grids, playful stationery clip art, heavy shadows, and any interface that hides the actual work behind decoration.

Positive references: Vercel for pure black, Geist and hairline grids; Linear for compact controls, restrained panels and dark-mode craft.

## Design Principles

1. The file is the interface. The preview and the ink controls get the space; chrome stays out of the way.
2. Chrome is monochrome. Color belongs to the ink choices and the document, never to decoration.
3. Structure is hairlines. One-pixel borders and spacing separate things; shadows are reserved for the paper itself.
4. Every state explains itself. Empty, processing, loaded, password and error states all say what is happening and what to do next.
5. Local-first is visible. Privacy is a fact shown in the footer and copy, not a badge we expect people to trust.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA contrast for text and controls. Visible keyboard focus on every control, including the drop zone. Ink colors always carry a name and a check mark, never color alone. Touch targets stay usable on mobile. Respect prefers-reduced-motion, including the processing bar.
